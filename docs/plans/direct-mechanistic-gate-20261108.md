# Proposed mechanism gate after activation controls

The user-selected `gpt-6.1-sol` surgeon, with `xhigh` reasoning, reviewed this
proposal after the provisional layers 16–23 effect was observed and **before any Stage 2 model scores**.
This is a separate conditional research plan. It does not change the frozen
14-forward experiment, choose weight coordinates or establish that its controls
passed. No geometry collection or candidate is admitted by this document alone.

The current observations cannot distinguish a faulty weight from a correct
component faithfully relaying an interpretation formed elsewhere. Even a full
activation-control pass would establish an information carrier, not a repair.

If and only if both fixed activation stages pass, freeze a new protocol:

1. Resolve the eight-layer winner through two four-layer halves, two two-layer
   halves of the qualifying half, then its two individual layers. Keep the same
   sufficiency threshold, dose and tie rule; include fresh neutral replay. At
   most seven additional forwards are proposed. If neither child qualifies,
   stop the single-layer repair track and record distributed or interacting
   carriage. Do not attribute the group's effect to an arbitrary matrix.
2. With authenticated weights, examine the resolved MLP's actual down-projection
   columns and input/output decomposition. Additional bounded observations must
   include MLP input and selected gate/up preactivations, verified against the
   down-projection input. The falsifiable claim is that a small feature set reads
   the erroneous empty-is-reversed interpretation and writes a harmful component
   at the first definition decision. An output contrast alone cannot show that.
3. Freeze the direction from the established causal contrast, without optimizing
   against labels or logits. Use a predeclared signed-column contribution and
   gate/up read-geometry rule. The proposed scope is one layer and no more than
   16 complete output columns: 40,960 selected BF16 positions. Before inspecting
   decomposition, require that these features account for at least half the
   positive projected semantic contrast. Diffuse geometry or broad syntax/style
   effects stop this sparse mechanism; do not expand support after results.
4. Intervene on the selected down-projection-input features before changing
   weights. Require predicted sufficiency, reverse effect and fixed matched
   support/magnitude controls. Freeze a practice family before scores: two
   half-open interval formulations where equality is valid and two explicitly
   positive-width formulations where equality is invalid. A component that
   simply suppresses `>=`, ignores the specification or makes comparisons
   universally strict fails specificity. These are exposed development cases.

Only after those gates could the surgeon review a particular algebraic removal
of an independently identified harmful write component, with its own frozen BF16
candidate form, strength, hashes, controls and regressions. It remains subject to
the existing sparsity/magnitude ceilings and protected tensors.

Solving `delta_W @ x = desired_delta_y`, choosing a minimum-norm update, matching
desired logits or tuning projection strength against observed scores does not
become acceptable because it is sparse, closed-form or avoids autograd. No LoRA,
optimizer, fitted probe, loss-fitting substitute or transferred 0.8B coordinate
is permitted. A later valid candidate would still need the separate
[paired acceptance boundary](../direct-paired-acceptance.md).
